---
name: android-companion
description: Use for the Android app that mirrors notifications, sends replies and transfers files to the PC.
---

- NotificationListenerService + RemoteInput reply; foreground service.
- Pair by QR, X25519 key exchange, encrypted LAN transport, no cloud.
- Guide the user to enable Notification Access and exempt from battery optimization.
- Large files: chunked transfer with resume and progress.
- iOS is files and clipboard only; never promise notifications on iOS.
